# Postmortem: <title>

Blameless: this documents what happened and what the system let happen, not
who did it.

## Summary

One or two sentences: what broke, for how long, how it was noticed.

## Impact

Who/what was affected, for how long, any numbers that matter (requests
failed, users affected, data lost).

- `HH:MM` — event

## Root cause

What actually caused it, not just the trigger. If there were multiple
contributing factors, list each.

## What went well

What caught this, limited the damage, or made the fix fast.

## What went wrong

What made this take longer than it should have, or let it happen at all.
